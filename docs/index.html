Who Can Volunteer?

Anyone passionate about education can join—students, professionals, and community members. Whether you can teach, mentor, or organize, there is a place for you at Agapay. No prior experience is required, just a willingness to help.

Benefits

Volunteering with Agapay allows you to make a real impact on children's literacy while developing your own skills in teaching, leadership, and community building. You will also gain valuable experience, meet like-minded people, and receive a certificate of appreciation for your service.

About Us

Mission

To empower underprivileged Filipino students by building their English reading and writing skills through volunteer-led sessions, and by directing requested monetary donations toward the cause. Agapay connects volunteers and donors into tangible action that helps students read with confidence, write with clarity, and believe in their own potential.

Vision

A Philippines where every student, regardless of economic background, can read and write in English which will open doors to education, opportunity, and in turn, prepare them to be internationally competent. A foundation where people come together to provide time and necessities to ensure that children are educated, confident, and advanced.

Objectives

Agapay is an initiative with a focused goal: to strengthen English literacy among Filipino youth through volunteer teaching and by responding to requests for monetary support related to the cause. To achieve this, the organization aims to:

  • To provide English reading and writing support to students in low-income communities by mobilizing volunteers to serve as tutors and reading partners.
  • To receive and evaluate requests for monetary assistance related to English literacy such as learning materials, facilities, or student opportunities and direct donations toward fulfilling them.
  • To distribute essential reading and writing tools such as books, workbooks, and school supplies
  • To create a safe and encouraging space for learning, where students feel comfortable practicing English without fear of judgment.
  • To raise awareness about the literacy gap in Philippine education and to inspire others to volunteer or donate.

Company History

  1. 2019

    Agapay started as a weekend reading circle in Metro Manila, where five volunteers tutored twelve children in a low-income community. These early sessions helped students build foundational English reading and writing skills in a safe, encouraging space.

  2. 2022

    Agapay expanded its volunteer network across Luzon and began directing requested donations toward learning materials, books, and school supplies, giving more Filipino students the tools they needed to learn.

  3. 2025

    Agapay launched its official website, connecting volunteers and donors nationwide. This scaled its impact, helping more students read with confidence, write with clarity, and believe in their own potential.

Our Impact

2,565 volunteers
352 scholars
780 books donated

Through six years, Agapay stayed true to its purpose. The organization continuously reached out to volunteers and sponsors, with the aim of gaining additional support to achieve their ultimate goal of opening up doors for children of all backgrounds through English education. They provided several events where children were able to connect with mentors and volunteers, helping spark their interest in areas such as creative writing and public speaking. Through those years, the organization had 2,565 volunteers, supported 352 scholars, and donated 780 books.

Agapay Launches "Bawat Bata, Bawat Kwento" Reading Drive

March 12, 2026

Agapay is proud to announce the launch of our newest initiative, the "Bawat Bata, Bawat Kwento" Reading Drive. This Metro Manila-wide project aims to bring 10,000 brand-new storybooks directly into the hands of children in public schools across Quezon City, Manila, Caloocan, and Pasay. Our goal is to spark a lifelong love for reading by ensuring every child has access to high-quality, age-appropriate literature.

We believe that literacy is the foundation of all future learning. By providing these books, we are not just giving away paper and ink; we are providing doors to new worlds, opportunities, and dreams. The campaign will run throughout the year, with distribution events scheduled quarterly. We invite our volunteers and community partners to join us in this massive effort to uplift Filipino youth.

150,000 Children Reached Nationwide

February 28, 2026

Thanks to the tireless dedication of our volunteers and the unwavering generosity of our partners, Agapay has officially touched the lives of over 150,000 children across Metro Manila. Since our inception, we have focused on building foundational English reading and writing skills in low-income communities, and this milestone proves that community-led interventions truly work.

This achievement belongs to everyone who has ever donated a book, spent a Saturday tutoring a child, or spread the word about our mission. While 150,000 is a massive number, we know there is still much work to be done. Moving forward, we are expanding our mentor training programs and opening new reading corners to reach even more out-of-school youth and struggling readers in the coming years.

New Partnership with Local Barangay Councils

February 10, 2026

Agapay has officially formalized partnerships with 40 barangay councils across Metro Manila. This collaboration is designed to establish weekly, permanent reading corners for out-of-school youth and children who need extra academic support. By utilizing local community spaces, we can provide a safe, accessible, and welcoming environment for learning right in the heart of the neighborhoods that need it most.

These barangay reading corners will be staffed by trained Agapay volunteers and will operate every weekend. This partnership allows us to be more deeply rooted in the communities we serve, ensuring that no child is left behind simply because they cannot travel to a central school. We look forward to working closely with local leaders to foster a culture of reading in every barangay.

Volunteer Orientation Kicks Off in Quezon City

January 22, 2026

Over 300 passionate new volunteers joined our Quezon City chapter last weekend to begin their training as reading mentors. The day was filled with workshops on child psychology, interactive storytelling techniques, and strategies for teaching English to struggling readers. It was inspiring to see so many students, professionals, and community members come together with a shared goal: to empower children through literacy.

Our new volunteers will be deployed to various reading centers across the city to work with children ages 5 to 12. They will be paired with small groups of learners to provide personalized attention and encouragement. If you missed this orientation, don't worry—our next batch of training sessions will open soon. Keep an eye on our social media pages for announcements.

Summer Reading Camp 2026 Registration Now Open

January 8, 2026

Keep your children engaged and learning this summer! Agapay is thrilled to announce that registration for our 6-week Summer Reading Camp is now officially open. This program is completely free of charge and is designed for children ages 6 to 12. We will be partnering with public schools across Metro Manila to host these fun-filled camps that combine reading, arts and crafts, and outdoor activities.

The camp focuses on making reading enjoyable through games, group storytelling, and creative writing exercises. It is a wonderful opportunity for children to build their confidence in English while making new friends. Slots are limited, so parents and guardians are encouraged to register their children as soon as possible. Let's make this summer a season of learning and discovery!

42,000 Books Donated This Year

December 15, 2025

As we close out the year, we are absolutely overwhelmed with gratitude. Our book donation drive has officially wrapped up, and thanks to the incredible generosity of our donors, we collected and distributed a record-breaking 42,000 books to children across Metro Manila. These books are now sitting on the shelves of our reading corners, in the hands of children who previously had nothing to read at home.

Each book represents a promise to a child that their potential is limitless. The joy on their faces during our distribution events was the perfect holiday gift for our entire team. We want to extend a massive thank you to every school, company, and individual who hosted a book drive or donated their gently used books. You have made a tangible difference in thousands of young lives.

New Books Arrive in Tondo

5 Simple Ways to Encourage Writing at Home

By Juan Dela Cruz · February 20, 2026

You don't need a classroom or a professional teacher to nurture a young writer. The home environment is actually the most powerful tool for developing a child's love for writing. First, create a "writing corner" with accessible paper and pencils. Second, encourage them to keep a daily journal, even if it's just a drawing with a single sentence. Third, play writing games like making up silly stories together.

Fourth, celebrate their efforts. Don't worry about spelling mistakes at first; focus on their ideas and creativity. Finally, let them see you write! When children see their parents writing grocery lists, emails, or letters, they understand that writing is a valuable, everyday skill. By making writing fun and low-pressure, you can help your child express themselves confidently.

Building a Reading Corner on a Small Budget

By Teacher Ana · January 3, 2026

You don't need thousands of pesos to create a magical reading space for children. It all starts with a cozy nook and a few creative choices. Start by clearing out a corner and adding a soft rug or floor cushions. Repurpose old crates or cardboard boxes to build bookshelves. The goal is to make the space feel welcoming, distinct from the rest of the house or classroom, and physically comfortable.

When it comes to books, second-hand stores, garage sales, and community book drives are your best friends. Ask neighbors for donations or start a book exchange. You can also print out free online storybooks. Decorate the walls with colorful student artwork. By prioritizing function over expensive aesthetics, you can build a reading corner that inspires children without breaking the bank.

The Power of Storytelling in the Classroom

By Teacher Mark · March 22, 2026

For children who struggle with traditional reading methods, oral storytelling can be a magical gateway to literacy. When a teacher or volunteer tells a story with animated voices, facial expressions, and gestures, it captures the child's imagination and builds listening comprehension skills. It removes the pressure of decoding words and allows the child to simply enjoy the narrative.

In our reading corners, we often start a session with a spoken story before introducing the book. By the time the child opens the book, they are already invested in the characters and eager to find out what happens next. This transition from listening to reading is a powerful tool that transforms reading from a difficult task into a highly anticipated adventure.
